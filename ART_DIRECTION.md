# The Safwa — First website direction

The approved brief calls for a premium, calm presentation joining The Safwa, Safwa Institute, Safwa Studios and the in-development Safwa Quran project. The parent hierarchy is a proposed presentation for review, not a statement of legal ownership or a signed partnership.

## Design

Warm cream, muted olive, navy within the Institute logo, forest green for Studios and restrained antique gold. Cormorant Garamond provides the editorial headings; DM Sans provides readable supporting text. Responsive composition uses fine rules, generous space and restrained motion. No generated photographs or stock-image substitutions were used.

## Content and contact

The Institute mission and vision are preserved verbatim from the supplied brand images. No courses, fees, application dates, testimonials, staff identities or audience numbers were invented. The Instagram accounts and WhatsApp community invitation are supplied by the user. General enquiries route to @thesafwa_; Studios and Quran enquiries route to @safwa.studios; learning, admissions and community questions route to @safwa.institute. The community invitation is presented separately and opens WhatsApp for the visitor to choose whether to join. No scholarship or download links were invented.

Safwa Quran is explicitly in development, with no download link or unverified feature claims. No existing Safwa Quran repository or other project was changed.

## Privacy and behaviour

This is a presentation website. The enquiry tool prepares a clipboard message and opens Instagram separately at the visitor's choice. It sends no application or enquiry data, stores no form entries, accepts no documents and includes no accounts or analytics. Clipboard failure reveals selectable text. Hosting infrastructure can process routine connection information.

## Build

Run `python scripts/build.py` to generate the five static pages. JavaScript and CSS are self-contained. Fonts are self-hosted with their OFL licenses in `dist/assets/licenses`. Browser QA covers navigation, the Institute's purpose tabs, the mobile menu, enquiry preparation and privacy dialog.
