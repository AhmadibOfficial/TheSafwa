About The Safwa

An institution taking shape.

The Safwa brings faith, knowledge, human development, creativity, media and technology into one long-term undertaking.

Why we exist

People. Ideas.
Work that lasts.

Founded by Khadija Sabiu, The Safwa exists to advance beneficial knowledge, develop human potential and create work that addresses meaningful needs.

That requires patient institution building, shared intellectual resources and systems that support the work beyond any single project.

Our vision

To build an enduring institution that advances knowledge, develops people, creates useful solutions and compounds beneficial impact across generations.

What we believe

Faith. Knowledge. Growth.

These commitments shape what we study, teach, write, create and build.
