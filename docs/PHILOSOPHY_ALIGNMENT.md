# The Safwa philosophy and public experience

The November 2026 correction follows an inspection of the Vercel homepage, About, Institute, Studios, Portfolio and its three division and concept records, From Safwa, Build With Us and Contact.

The public narrative places faith, knowledge and growth at the centre of institution building. It connects education, scholarship, media and technology through the development of people and useful ideas over time. The parent institution provides that shared context. Each arm has a distinct role.

| Public experience | Direction applied |
| --- | --- |
| Parent | Institutional purpose, conviction, ecosystem and the long view |
| Institute | Education, knowledge, research, learning and community, with its approved mission and vision |
| Studios | Media, publishing, products and technology |
| Safwa OS | A concept within the Studios technology direction |
| Safwa Quran | An existing project in development under Studios, as required by the SAD |
| Acquisition | A future enterprise direction |
| From Safwa | Essays, reflections and research that appear after an editorial decision |
| The work | Published evidence of work, released through the editorial workflow |
| Build With Us | Contribution categories and actual openings when reviewed and published |
| Contact | A direct route to the appropriate institutional team |

The earlier seeded journal note, project list and contribution records were incorrectly presented as published material. This revision creates private editorial drafts on a fresh database and leaves the public editorial collections empty. Institution and division descriptions are separate from claims of completed work.

The public email addresses were observed on the reference Contact page. The local enquiry tool prepares an email for the visitor to review and send. The existing Vercel form and CMS internals have not been inspected.

The private browser preview uses a static projection of this reviewed baseline. Local setup and download instructions are available on the separate local setup page. The public institutional pages contain the brand and visitor journeys.

The subsequent explicit instruction to build the SQLite CMS selects the implementation in SQLITE_CMS.md. The existing Vercel CMS internals remain uninspected; this implementation preserves the approved public experience and does not migrate unseen Vercel data. Publishing permissions and recovery still require institutional acceptance before production cutover.
